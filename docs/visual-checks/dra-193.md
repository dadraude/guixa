Coverage per view, exactly:

- Every populated settings page and role: 375 and 1280 px in both themes at 100%. The manager role
  adds 375 px/200% and 1024 px/200% in both themes.
- Every authentication page populated: the same four 100% cases plus 375 px/200% in both themes and
  1024 px/200% in light.
- Regression views (Orders index and edit, Dashboard, Producer Cutoff, manager Cycles): 375 and
  1280 px in both themes at 100% plus 1024 px/200% in light; Orders index and edit add 1024 px/200%
  dark, 375 px/200% in both themes and 1024 px/100% (the width nearest the row container threshold)
  in both themes, and their extra states use the combinations declared above.
  Cutoff, Cycles and Orders edit add a 375 px/200% error state in both themes. Dashboard, Cutoff and
  Cycles are not declared at 375 px/200% populated.
- Error, processing and status states use 375 px/200% or the theme where their colour is at risk.
  Portals (delete, setup, confirmation and remove dialogs) add 1280 px dark where declared.

Portalled dialog states set `root` and `panels` to the dialog content and its children (excluding
the Close control) and wait for the dialog, so the harness measures the dialog and not the page
behind it.

Content probe exclusions (all other headings, labels, inputs, buttons, links, paragraphs and
spans are measured): `h1.sr-only`; labels that are `.sr-only` or contain `.sr-only`; `span.sr-only`;
inputs that are `type="hidden"` or `aria-hidden="true"` (the Radix checkbox bubble input; the
OTP overlay input is measured); buttons with a direct `.sr-only` child (the sidebar trigger, the dialog Close, icon buttons);
any link containing `.sr-only` (for example the logo link). Continuous animation (the spinner and OTP caret) is excluded
from the settled-capture selector by design, and the processing states record that moving content
remains.

## Acceptance checks outside the shared harness

`check.mjs` drives real Chrome key events and computed styles: 208 checks, all passing.

- **Contrast:** every visible text element at least 4.5:1 (3:1 for large text), input and
  checkbox boundaries at least 3:1, and the scoped focus ring at least 3:1, on 18 page/state
  combinations at 375 and 1280 px in light and dark, including the dialogs, the setup modal, the
  selected settings navigation item (exactly one per settings page) and the Orders rows.
  The probe did not measure placeholder text or hover and pressed states.
- **Setup modal QR:** a real-sized (192 × 192, as Fortify emits) QR SVG stays square, at least
  160 px (or 90% of the dialog content where narrower), inside every clipping ancestor and the
  dialog content edges at 375/100, 375/200 and 1280 px in both themes; the "or" separators in the
  dialog, Login and Confirm password carry their own surface colour.
- **Targets:** every visible button, link and input is at least 44 × 44 px on the same
  combinations. Radix checkboxes keep their 20 px control inside a 44 px label row.
- **Appearance:** the real Appearance page selects Light, Dark and System, stores
  `localStorage.appearance` and the `appearance` cookie, survives reload, and System follows the
  emulated OS in both directions.
- **Keyboard submission:** pressing Enter in the focused field submits login, forgot password,
  reset password, confirm password, accept invitation, recovery code, OTP, profile and password
  update forms once through the native form.
- **Welcome:** no `.pilot-theme` element, its original page background in both appearances and no
  theme token on the document root; `Welcome.vue` is byte-identical to the base.
- **Unthemed consumers:** `InputError.vue` and the shared UI primitives are unchanged, so the
  unthemed pickup and reception pages that render `InputError` keep their classes. The new rules
  are scoped under `.pilot-theme`.

## Issues found and fixed

The first shared sweeps found and fixed: unscrollable tall dialogs and an off-screen first focus
inside them; the settings navigation sitting beside the content at 1024 px/200%; panel padding
leaving no content width at 375 px/200%; a nowrap passkey button and a Delete account button
escaping their cards; six OTP slots wider than the phone; a collapsed recovery-code block that
remained focusable; 36 px logo and "log in" links; 41 px copy and delete targets; 2.3:1 passkey
separators; 3.5:1 white text on the dark destructive button; and literal red, green and neutral
colours. Each was reproduced in the harness or acceptance checks before the fix.
The first review round added: setup-modal overflow at 375 px/200% (QR width, OTP slots, button row,
dialog padding), dialog states that measured nothing because they were portalled, the selected
settings navigation that never rendered in the fixture (it used a fake URL), and a 0.5rem settings
gutter at normal text. The red reproductions are the dialog subset run on the previous HEAD and
`check.mjs` (selected navigation and gutter assertions) run on it. The second review round added:
the QR frame overflowing the dialog content at 375 px/200% and the dimensionless fixture SVG that
hid it, separator labels painted with the card colour on the dialog surface, and the Orders row
focus and contrast checks.
