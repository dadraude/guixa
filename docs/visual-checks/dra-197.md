## Static check

`resources/js/layout-rules.spec.ts` scans the class strings of `checkedFiles` and fails on a spacing
utility (`p*`, `m*`, `gap*`, `space-*`) outside Tailwind `0`, `px`, `auto`, `1`, `2`, `4`, `6`, `8`,
`12`, and on arbitrary `w/h/size/min/max` values; variant prefixes and negative margins are handled.
**Known limit:** step `1` is allowed everywhere, because a static check cannot tell a single control
from a container; review and the rendered check cover that. `components/ui/` and `Welcome.vue` are
excluded and a test asserts it. Later slices append their files to `checkedFiles`.

## Findings

- Review round 1 (pattern: money, quantity and row definitions): four pickup-component amounts were neither end-aligned nor tabular, three lists and the pickup total rebuilt the shared rules inline, a card hover was defined twice and an adjustment quantity floated mid-row. The sweep covered every money value and list on both pages, and `check.mjs` now measures them all.
- Before the fixes the first sweep found a pickup total whose amount escaped its panel at
  375 px/200%, an amount that wrapped to the start edge (not end-aligned), a sidebar with 4 px between
  44 px items, and a sidebar-open case whose first focus target was covered by the sheet.
  Each was fixed and the case rerun.
- **Pre-existing, outside T43 (`Manage/Pickups/Show`, reverted pickup components give the same
  result):** at 375 px/200% its delivered-quantity form pushes Save outside the viewport and its line list
  escapes its container; its inputs and buttons are 32–36 px high; its link is 20 px high; and at
  1024 px/200% the manager sidebar's 11 items overlap its footer. They belong to T41 and the page
  stays unthemed. The regression case is therefore declared at 1280 px, where the pickup components
  pass.
- **Manager pickup at 375 px/200%:** the shared harness cannot take a case here, because the page's own delivered-quantity form (`FORM.flex items-center gap-2`, untouched) overflows (pre-existing, for T45 to fix). Instead `check.mjs` has a `pickup-lists` probe that runs only on the stock, return and adjustment lists on that page, at 375 px/200% (light and dark) and 1280 px (light and dark): 4 more checks, all passing. They measure tabular figures, end alignment and row end edges of every amount.
- **Divider colour (changed):** the pickup lists now divide rows with `var(--border)` (`.pilot-list > li + li`), where `divide-y` used the default gray. Pickup lists on the unthemed manager page therefore have a slightly different divider, mostly visible in dark mode. Kept on purpose, one definition for both pages.
- **Pending:** the user's explicit visual approval; real server round trips (covered by the
  existing Pest tests, not re-run since no PHP changed).
