Task: DRA-213

T58 / DRA-213 implements R23, R31 and R32 amount-only corrections while retaining the S85 receipt-correction regression. The public CorrectBasket command appends signed nonzero corrections to validated independent baskets. Sales owns basket_adjustments; one transaction records its correction identity as a basket-linked Wallet correction. Matching identity/content replay has one effect; conflicting replay fails. Original validation total, line snapshots, stock and collection remain unchanged. Finance/Manager can correct; household members see original/effective totals and ordered history without a write path.

Plan clarification: a zero-total validated basket has no original charge, so basketId itself supplies the accepted original basket source. The effective basket total is the validated total minus signed Wallet correction amounts. Replay compares basket, actor, signed amount and trimmed reason. This specifies the existing basket-source design without changing product requirements or adding physical return/collection correction behavior. Existing receipt and Ordering cycle correction contracts remain supported.
