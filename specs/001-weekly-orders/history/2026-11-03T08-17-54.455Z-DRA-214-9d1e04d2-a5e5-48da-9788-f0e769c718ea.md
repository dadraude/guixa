# Q&A — Reopening a reception after baskets are validated (T59)

Asked while implementing T59 (DRA-214), on 2026-11-03. The implementer found that keeping a validated
household's allocation while subtracting its effective quantity unbalances allocations and blocks R18
distribution (received 7; A allocated 4 with actual 3; B 4; total 8).

**Q (step 8: what can happen when a reception is reopened?)**
A: "en el pas 8 només pot passar que arribi un productor que encara no havia vingut o que en repartir hi hagués una confusió i s'hagués de repartir de nou. En el primer cas s'afegirà a la cistella. En el segon cas es modificarà la cistella amb els nous valors."

**Q (P1: drop the "unvalidated only" choice and always apply to all baskets?)**
A: "si són productes nous afegits, es crea una cistella nova si l'anterior ja estava validada. si és ajustar quantitats, no es podrà fer si hi ha cistelles validades ja que segurament ho hauran recollit ja."

**Q (P2: block only when a validated basket contains that product?)**
A: "Només si en té" — block when a validated basket has a line of the product or would change.

**Q (P3: is a late arrival a product reopened from received 0 to more?)**
A: "Sí, rebut 0 → >0".

**Q (P4: is the new basket a weekly basket of the same cycle, validated/charged separately and gating closure?)**
A: "Setmanal, bloqueja tancament".

**Q (P5: later late arrivals add to an open weekly draft instead of creating another basket?)**
A: "Afegir a l'esborrany".

**Q (P6: a blocked redistribution is resolved with existing per-basket corrections, T68/T58?)**
A: "Sí, correccions T68/T58".

**Q (P7: spec change in the same T59 PR, with explicit OK per phase?)**
A: "Mateix PR, OK per fase".
