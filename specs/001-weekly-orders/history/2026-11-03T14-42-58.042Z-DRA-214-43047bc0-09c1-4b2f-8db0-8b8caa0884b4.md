# Q&A — Hand allocation to a fully validated household (T59)

Asked after independent review 3 of T59 (DRA-214) on 2026-11-03 (.atl review-3, Q1).

**Q: No validated basket holds a product (e.g. cherries). After a reopen, a manager hand-allocates 1 kg to H, who never ordered it and whose weekly baskets are all validated. Create/extend H's late weekly basket, only if the product arrived in this reopen, or reject except for newly added products?**
A: "Always a late basket" — create or extend H's late weekly basket whenever no validated basket contains the product; the block applies only when a validated basket has a line of it (Q&A 1431705f).
