## 2026-11-04T23:23:45.847Z · Deviation

Task: DRA-217

T62 (R35a, S43, S58, S59). Membership's household settlement no longer reads pending pickups from Ordering. The `HouseholdPickups` port and its Ordering adapter are replaced by `HouseholdBaskets`, whose `SalesHouseholdBaskets` adapter calls the new Sales internal query `ReadOpenHouseholdBaskets` (no actor; the manager-only Membership use cases already authorise).

Deviation 1, module graph: the Membership to Sales dependency (Sales is now an owner called by Membership for settlement, in addition to `ReadActiveHouseholds` in the other direction) was not in the plan. `architecture/modules.php` and `docs/architecture/modules.md` gain the `Sales -> Membership` export (`ReadOpenHouseholdBaskets`, `OpenBasketData`) and Membership lists Sales as a dependency. The `Ordering -> Membership` export of `ReadPendingPickups` and `PendingPickupData` is dropped; Dashboard still uses them from `app/`.

Deviation 2, scope of "pending": the settlement list and the deactivation warning now cover every Sales basket (weekly, stock and return) that is draft or validated with goods still to collect (`Basket::isPendingCollection`), reported as basket id, status and date, instead of one entry per pickup cycle. The HTTP row `PendingPickupRow` is replaced by `OpenBasketRow`, `HouseholdSettlementRow.pendingPickups` becomes `openBaskets`, and the household settlement UI links each open basket to the Finance basket page. The three visual fixtures that hardcode the settlement (`dra-205-visual.ts`, `dra-198-visual.ts`, `reference-all-pages-visual.ts`) received the same rename only.

Deviation 3, tracker mismatch: the T62 row in `tasks.md` lists dependencies T59 and T32, while the task detail also names T64 and T66. Tracker relations were not changed.
