# Deviation — `ListStockMovements` also returns the product header (T76)

Task: DRA-238 (T76). Requirement: R28a (history stays available after the product is deactivated).

The plan's `ListStockMovements` row returned only `StockMovementLine`s and the total. The Finance and
producer history pages need the product's name, unit and current available stock, and `ListStock`
hides deactivated products from Finance, so the header could not come from it without breaking R28a.
The page now also carries `productName`, `unit` and `available` (the newest line's balance). The
plan's Stock table row was updated; no requirement changed.
