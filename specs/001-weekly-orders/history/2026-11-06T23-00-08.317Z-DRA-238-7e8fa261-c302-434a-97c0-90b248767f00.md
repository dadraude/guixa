# Q&A — Should Finance's stock list show deactivated products? (T76)

Date: 2026-11-06. Source: the user, during the T76 review (DRA-238).

Question: Should `/finance/stock` also list deactivated stock products, so Finance can reach the
history of a product that is no longer offered (R28a)?

Answer (user): Yes. List them marked inactive, each with its History link. The producer list already
works this way.

selection and every other caller still get active stock only; a Manager always sees all). The Finance
controller passes it. Plan `## HTTP & pages` row for `/finance/stock` updated. No requirement text
