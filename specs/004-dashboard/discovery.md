## Problem and outcome

The dashboard should first answer “What needs my attention now?” with work the signed-in person can perform. It should also show ordering or other opportunities without calling them obligations, and later show relevant insights. The outcome is a quick, trustworthy route into existing task screens for a volunteer, household member, or producer with one or several roles. This supports the whole-product discovery's short volunteer sessions and low-confidence phone use.

**Audiences:** household members, producers, Manager, Finance, Catalogue, and Receiver. Relevance follows current household membership, producer association, and staff permissions; shared permitted work counts even when no individual assignment exists. Combined roles yield one overview, with duplicate work shown once. Links lead to existing authorized task screens, where work is completed.

**Settled presentation rules:** pending work comes before opportunities, then insights; work with a real deadline precedes other pending work. A pickup date alone does not make work overdue. Group similar work with exact counts and identifying context. A person with no pending work gets a clear caught-up state while opportunities and insights remain available. Missing history gets an explanation, not a zero or invented trend. These rules are cross-slice invariants, including if an insight is added later.

**Cross-cycle scope (Q16):** Include all permitted pending work across cycles with identifying context. A basket from a closed cycle remains visible while actionable; passing its scheduled date or closing its cycle alone does not hide it.

**Opportunity eligibility (Q15):** Catalogue maintenance and reviewing a producer’s sent-order summary are opportunities unless an existing workflow state identifies specific unfinished work. An inactive product may be intentional; a sent summary alone is not an unfinished producer task. Do not turn either into permanent pending work.

**Pending action groups (Q17):** Members collect their household’s validated baskets awaiting collection. Receiver enters received quantities and finishes allocations. Finance receives and allocates goods, validates and distributes receptions, prepares and validates baskets, and records collection. Manager has that operational work plus manual sending of eligible producer orders. Q20 corrects Q17: cycle closure is automatic under 001 R24b and is not a pending dashboard action. Existing workflow eligibility governs every action. Exclude scheduler-owned empty orders, baskets with nothing to collect, and completed work. Combined permissions do not duplicate the same work. Catalogue maintenance and producer summary review remain opportunities under Q15.

**Reception stages (Q21):** Unvalidated receptions invite checking delivery and allocations, with validation available to Finance/Manager. Allocation differences that block distribution require resolution. Validated receptions ready for distribution invite Finance/Manager to mark them distributed. Received totals and allocations start from order defaults (001 R14), so balance alone does not prove checking. Use existing validation rather than adding a confirmation state. Receiver sees only permitted review/allocation work.

**Ordering without deadlines (Q19):** For pending work with no actual deadline, earlier scheduled dates precede later dates; undated work follows dated work. Same-date work follows sending orders → reception → baskets → collection (Q20 removes the previously proposed manual cycle-closure step), with deterministic remaining ties. A scheduled date still does not imply overdue status. Actual deadlines keep Q7’s priority.

**Freshness (Q18):** Refresh when the dashboard opens or the person returns to it. Offer explicit refresh and show last-updated time. Data remains unchanged while the page stays open until refreshed; there is no background refresh in this slice. A stale item’s destination shows current state and applies current permissions/workflow rules.

**Settled insight directions:** household spending and ordering changes; producer demand by product; Manager cycle progress and waiting work; Finance basket validation/collection plus wallet balances and recent receipts; Catalogue explicit product availability; Receiver reception and remaining allocation. Meanings constrain future specs: spending uses actual validated-basket charges including corrections, while ordered estimates and wallet top-ups stay separate; wallet receipts do not imply a per-basket paid state; producer demand is requested quantities in sent producer orders in each product's unit, with still-changeable orders marked provisional; availability can show inactive or cycle-unavailable products, but no undefined “catalogue gap” threshold. Insight measures and presentation beyond these decisions remain to be specified in their selected slice.

## Boundaries and dependencies

- Read-only dashboard information and navigation into existing work. It creates no new domain state, write command, deadline, notification, reminder, payment allocation, producer invoice, or analytics platform.
- Household order and Sales basket are different records. A basket charge is recorded on validation; physical collection is separate. A wallet receipt is an independent credit. Never infer an unpaid basket from a negative wallet balance.
- Receiver may open and change receptions and allocations but cannot validate, distribute, or reopen them. Finance and Manager can run those steps. Catalogue controls product availability within its accepted role; a producer sees only its own context. Current authorization governs both item visibility and destination access.
- Preserve the independently shipped login destination from retired 002 T05/R11 and the interim 001 T61 dashboard summary responsibility. The new slice can replace the interim summary when approved; it does not take ownership of those prior tasks.
- Roadmap work shifts, notifications, producer confirmation, minimum order rules, invoices, and producer payments are future features. Dashboard copy must reflect only workflow states and deadlines that exist when each slice is delivered.

## Candidate delivery slices

1. **Unified attention and opportunities — selected first (Q14).** For every currently available permission context, show actionable shared or personal work in a single grouped, deduplicated, priority-ordered overview, plus distinct opportunities and a caught-up state. Each item links to its existing authorized list/detail screen; real deadlines alone establish urgency. Complete when accounts with multiple roles see each eligible work group once, each audience reaches its work, unavailable work stays hidden, and empty/current states are clear. Depends on completed 001 and any 003 role behavior the slice promises; confirm 003 delivery before basing acceptance on Catalogue or Receiver. Breadth is intentional because deduplication, priority, and permission relevance are one user-facing promise across roles. If 003 is not delivered, the selected spec must either wait for it or explicitly stage its two new roles without claiming full-audience completion.
2. **Household and Finance insight context.** Explain actual charges and household ordering changes, and show Finance wallet balances/recent receipts beside basket validation and collection context. Complete when estimates, validated charges, corrections, receipts, and physical collection are labelled distinctly and sparse history is explained. Depends on slice 1 for placement and 001 Sales/Wallet data.
3. **Producer demand insight.** Show requested quantities by product and unit from sent producer orders, with provisional current-order context where applicable. Complete when users cannot confuse requested with received quantity or producer payment, and cross-producer/household data stays private. Depends on slice 1 and 001 producer-order history; later producer confirmation and invoicing do not silently change this meaning.
4. **Operational and catalogue insights.** Add Manager cycle progress/waiting work, Receiver reception/allocation progress, and Catalogue explicit availability states. Complete when each stage has a clear denominator and current state, role access is respected, and undefined “gaps” or invented alarms are absent. Depends on slice 1 and delivered 003 roles/reception powers; future roadmap workflows would need their own later decision.

**Why slice 1 first:** it delivers the agreed primary purpose and tests the uncertain shared-role model before any chart or metric expands the page. It also gives the later insights a stable place and priority order. Q14 accepts this first-slice boundary. It does not accept detailed requirements, implementation, or the exact sequence of later insight slices.
