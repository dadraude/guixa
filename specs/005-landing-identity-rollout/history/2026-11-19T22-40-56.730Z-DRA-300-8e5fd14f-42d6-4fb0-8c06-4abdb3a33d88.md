**Resolution.** The select control and the unmigrated-page probes are re-pointed to `Wallet/Statement` (green on the base before the page rows, green after). Wallet panels use `.pilot-layout-panel:not(:has(.pilot-wallet-movements))` and the control view measures headings and labels only, as the Wallet manifests of slice 2 do, because the movements table is out of scope for the control.
