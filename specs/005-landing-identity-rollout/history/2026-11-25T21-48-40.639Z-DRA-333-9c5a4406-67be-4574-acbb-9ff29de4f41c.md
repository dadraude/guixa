## Reading rules

- Dimensions: **A** = 375 and 1280 px at 100% text and 1024 px at 200% text, each light and dark. **N** = 375 px light and dark. **W** = 1280 px at 100% and 1024 px at 200%, light and dark. An item takes the dimensions of the page state or overlay it sits on; an element not rendered at a width is stated as not applicable there, never as met.
- Text (R5): 4.5:1, or 3:1 for large text (24 px, or 18.66 px bold). Non-text (R5): 3:1 against the colour outside the edge or indicator. Target (R6): box of 44 x 44 px or more. Focus visible (R6): a real Tab or the overlay's own keys reach that instance and an indicator shows. Layout (R7): inside the viewport, unclipped, no horizontal page scroll.
- Disabled controls: their text and boundary contrast is not graded (inactive components, WCAG 1.4.3 and 1.4.11); their state, size and layout are.
- Meaningful divider: a line that separates two records or two groups of menu commands. Not meaningful here, so not listed: outer edges of a list, panel, popup, menu, sheet or toast, and the app bar and sidebar edges. Filled buttons are identified by their text and fill; no border item.
- A C3 manifest focus list requires only one reached element per selector, so focus items name every instance.
- Conditions the three C3 manifests already assert are not repeated below; they are re-run unchanged, and item G-1 adds their missing dimensions.
